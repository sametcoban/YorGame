# Chapter encounters

Twelve chapters, five stages each: 60 sequential stages and 180 waves. Story rewards recruit missing named heroes until all 30 are collected. Old chapter-six completion continues at chapter seven; campaign restart is removed.

Chapters 7–12 use two or three simultaneous enemies in regular waves. Every chapter from chapter 3 onward ends with two bosses together. Enemy Preview shows exact wave compositions and roles. New themed variants use the bundled animated reference models; build them with **Ashlight > Art > Build Enemy Roster**.

The table lists reference enemies and base health before party-size and wave composition scaling. For chapters 7–12, regular enemies use 22% of their reference health in early waves and 55% in final waves. Chapter-ending bosses use their full reference health and 70% for the partner. A five-slot party scales enemy health by 5/3, rounded up.
